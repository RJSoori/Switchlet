# Switchlet

A private file converter that runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device: nothing is uploaded, there's no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.

## What it can do

**Photos**
- Convert between HEIC, JPG, PNG, WebP and AVIF
- Resize and compress, with a before/after view of the size difference
- Remove hidden details like GPS location, camera model and dates, and see a list of exactly what was removed

**PDFs**
- Merge, split and reorder pages
- Turn images into a PDF, or PDF pages into images
- Compress, and remove hidden author and edit details

**Documents and spreadsheets**
- Markdown ↔ HTML
- CSV ↔ JSON ↔ Excel

**Audio and video**
- Convert between MP4, MOV and WebM
- Trim clips
- Pull the audio out of a video
- Turn a video into a GIF

## Made to come back to

- **Recipes:** save the settings you use often and run them again with one drop. You can share a recipe as a link.
- **History:** a list of your recent conversions, kept only on your device and cleared with one click.
- **Works offline:** after your first visit, Switchlet keeps working without an internet connection.
- **Share from your phone:** on Android, send files to Switchlet straight from the share menu.
- **Keyboard friendly:** press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on Mac) for quick commands.

## Why you can trust it

Most online converters upload your files to their servers. Switchlet does all the work inside your browser instead. You don't have to take our word for it: the privacy panel counts every byte sent from the page, and it stays at zero. You can also check for yourself in your browser's developer tools, under the Network tab.

## How to use it

1. Drop a file anywhere on the page, paste it, or pick one.
2. Choose what you want it to become.
3. Download the result.
